TL;DR

SPHERE studies why MoE policies lose plasticity in continual RL, diagnoses the drop through spectral collapse in the empirical NTK, and regularizes weighted expert features to keep update geometry better conditioned.

Phenomenon → diagnosis → regularization
Problem Continual RL can make later-task updates effectively low-dimensional.
Method A practical Parseval penalty on weighted expert features.
Effect Higher spectral plasticity, stronger continual-control performance, and clearer feature usage across tasks.
Why Policies Stop Learning in Continual RL
Phenomenon In continual RL, policies keep receiving new experience but can stop changing effectively.
Diagnosis The eNTK effective rank drops, showing that updates concentrate into too few functional directions.
SPHERE SPHERE adds a practical Parseval penalty on weighted expert features to keep update directions more diverse.

Update Geometry: Collapse vs. SPHERE

The visualization makes the diagnosis concrete. A unit sphere of possible gradient directions becomes an ellipsoid after multiplication by the eNTK matrix $\mathbf{K}$. When $\mathbf{K}$ becomes low-rank, one axis shrinks toward zero and the ellipsoid degenerates toward a near-plane or line; SPHERE keeps the spectrum more isotropic.

Experiments & Analysis

The story follows the paper: first show the continual-RL failure mode, then diagnose spectral collapse, then present SPHERE’s results and supporting analyses.

Phenomenon · Diagnosis · Results · Ablations
Phenomenon
Phenomenon: Continual RL Degrades Across Architectures
Before introducing SPHERE, the same HumanoidBench architectures lose success under continual RL (CRL) compared to per-task RL.
Diagnosis
Diagnosis: Spectral Plasticity Collapses During CRL
The performance drop is accompanied by lower $r_e(\mathbf{K})$: baselines lose spectral plasticity, while SPHERE keeps the update geometry better conditioned.
Result
Result: SPHERE Narrows the Continual-RL Gap on MetaWorld
On MetaWorld, SPHERE narrows the gap between per-task RL and continual RL, retaining the strongest average success among the compared methods.
Result
Result: SPHERE Also Improves HumanoidBench
On HumanoidBench, the same pattern holds: SPHERE improves average success over unregularized MoE and the continual-learning baselines.
Ablation

Mechanism
Mechanism: Weighted Expert Features Track $r_e(\mathbf{K})$
The weighted expert-feature Gram tracks the eNTK effective-rank trend, giving a practical target for preserving spectral plasticity.
Qualitative
Qualitative: Feature Collapse Across Tasks
Using fixed held-out Stair states, we visualize last-layer routing-weighted expert features after each task. Without SPHERE, points quickly concentrate on one dominant singular direction; with SPHERE, multiple directions remain active across the sequence.
